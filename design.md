# WILD MAC — DEFINITIVE HOMEPAGE HERO SPECIFICATION (`design.md`)

## 01 — THE DEFINITIVE HERO CONCEPT

**Creative Positioning:** An editorial physical book still-life built around the author's published library and worldview — embodying *Contemporary Independent Author House × Editorial Magazine × Physical Book Object × Quiet Luxury*.

### Key Design Tenets:
1. **Books as Primary Visual Subject**: The 4 authentic hardcover book covers visually behave like physical objects on an open publishing desk rather than digital thumbnails or cards.
2. **Asymmetric 45/55 Layout**:
   - **Left (45%)**:
     - `WM / AUTHOR • WRITER • ADVISOR`
     - Headline: **“A life worth living deserves ideas worth carrying.”** (Large Cormorant Garamond display serif)
     - Supporting copy: *“Books, reflections, and practical perspectives on life, purpose, money, business, and what comes next.”*
     - Flat CTAs: `Explore the Books →` + `Meet the Author →`
     - Lower left chapter marker: `01 / THE LIBRARY // FOUR VOLUMES`
   - **Right (55%)**:
     - Authentic physical still life of all 4 books:
       1. *A Letter To My Daughter* (Hero foreground book, dominant scale, -1.5° subtle angle, realistic 3D paper spine and crease highlight).
       2. *The Path Of Purpose* (Layered behind right, +5° subtle angle).
       3. *The Sacred Path* (Layered midground left, -6° subtle angle).
       4. *Financial Literacy* (Layered bottom right, +1° angle).
     - **Open-Book Environmental Spread**: Dual-page folio spread underlay creating the feeling of *"a book becoming an environment."*
3. **Restrained Red Signature**:
   - 1–2% of total canvas (tiny 6px marker, 1px rules, active link accents).
4. **Mobile Recomposition (375px–430px)**:
   - Deliberately composed so that the brand marker, headline, physical book stack, and CTAs sit directly in the first viewport without 2×2 grid distortion.
